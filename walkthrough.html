Walkthrough & Tips

Strategies and advice for conquering the dungeon

Early Game Guide

First 10 Loops

  • ► Focus entirely on Max HP upgrades
  • ► Don't risk gold on mid-run purchases
  • ► Push to Floor 5+ for better gold
  • ► Ignore equipment quality, just equip highest ilvl
  • ► Death is expected - learn enemy patterns

Key Milestones

  • ► Loop 5: First boss encounter
  • ► Loop 10: Unlock second upgrade tree
  • ► Loop 25: Access to rare equipment
  • ► Loop 50: Epic items start dropping
  • ► Loop 100: Endgame content unlocked

Floor Progression Strategy

Floors 1-5
Rush through quickly. Monsters are weak. Collect gold and avoid risky fights. Rest at every opportunity.
Floors 6-15
Monsters get dangerous. Fight only when you have full health. Prioritize HP upgrades between runs.
Floors 16-25
Consider specializing in one upgrade tree. Gold scaling really kicks in here - you should have 500+ gold per run.
Floors 26-50
Mid-game. Balance offense and defense. Bosses every 10 floors become major hurdles - prepare properly.
Floors 50+
Endgame territory. Focus on damage output to clear faster. Defense matters less when you oneshot enemies.

Boss Battle Strategies

Every 10 Floors: Mini-Boss

  • • 3× normal monster HP
  • • Special attack every 5 turns
  • • Higher gold/xp reward
  • Tip: Clear all other rooms first for bonus stats

Floor 50: First Major Boss

  • • 10× normal monster HP
  • • Multiple attack phases
  • • Guaranteed epic drop
  • Tip: Have 2000+ HP and 50+ damage before attempting

Survival Tips

Defensive

  • • Never fight below 30% HP
  • • Always check room before entering
  • • Keep 20% gold reserve
  • • Exit stairs are always accessible

Offensive

  • • Prioritize targets: healers > ranged > melee
  • • One-shot enemies before they act
  • • Save burst abilities for bosses
  • • Area damage beats single target at scale

Economic

  • • Gold in bank > gold in pocket
  • • Equipment sells for 50% of value
  • • Bulk selling saves time
  • • Rare items worth keeping for collection

Common Mistakes to Avoid

✗

Spending all gold mid-run instead of banking it

✗

Ignoring Max HP upgrades in early loops

✗

Fighting when health is low "just one more room"

✗

Spreading upgrades across all trees evenly

✗

Selling rare quality equipment

✗

Skipping rest rooms when wounded
