Recommended Upgrade Priority

1-10

Max Vitality first. 10 levels = 150 extra HP. Prioritize survival.

11-25

Mix Vitality + Strength. 5 each. Start building damage.

26-50

Add Toughness and Luck. 5 each. You're now tanky enough to push deeper.

51-100

Heavy Strength investment. 20+ levels. One-shot meta begins.

100+

Balanced build. Discovery for collection. Luxury upgrades.
